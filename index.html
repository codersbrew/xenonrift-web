SOL ALLIANCE / APHELION COLLECTIVE

Small fighter. Big battle.

↗

Break the enemy fleet

Each faction fields a carrier and two or three destroyers armed with heavy main guns and point-defense turrets. Strip enemy turrets, wreck the carrier’s launch bays, or drain its 100-fighter reserve so their pilots must warp in from their base Xenon Rift with double the respawn wait. Destroy every enemy capital ship to win the rift. No match clock.

↗

Move the front line

Choose Sol Alliance or Aphelion Collective once per Rift Cycle—your faction holds until a capital falls and the galaxy re-forms. Fight across 15 rifts in the Orion Spur, from Earth and Titan to Aphelion’s red-dwarf worlds. Pick an open front on the galactic map and drop into its battle. Victories capture and lock rifts, opening adjacent fronts.

↗

Learn offline

Practice a 12v12 Fleet Battle, Team Deathmatch, or a full Offline Rift War against bots without an account or server connection. Bots fly a mix of all three classes and fill empty online fighter slots too. Track fleets and reserves on the fleet-strip HUD, read shields and hull on ship rings, and hear the ship-computer announcer call out threats.

Can I play without other players?

Yes. Practice runs offline against bots without signing in—either a single 12v12 battle or a full Offline Rift War across the galaxy. Online, bots fill open fighter slots so you can join even when few people are playing.

Does the online galaxy keep going when I leave?

Yes. The dedicated galaxy saves its war state in the database and runs battles with bots while pilots are away. Your game does not need to stay open. Offline practice is separate from the shared online war.

How does progression work?

Current development builds track battle score, assists, and a war rank for each Rift Cycle. The galactic map can summarize changes since your last visit. These newer online features are still being verified for playtests; offline practice does not affect your online career.

What if I cannot join?

Use the current playtest build and check the galaxy status shown on the Rift War button in the main menu. If a front closes after its battle or a connection drops, return to the map or menu and join an open front. Follow the first-launch instructions supplied with your build.

Need help or want to report a bug?

Ask for help ↗. Include your device, build version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.
