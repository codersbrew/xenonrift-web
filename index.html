SOL ALLIANCE / ZHURAK HEGEMONY

Small fighter. Big battle.

↗

Break the enemy fleet

Each faction fields a carrier and two or three destroyers armed with heavy main guns and point-defense turrets. Strip enemy turrets, wreck the carrier’s launch bays, or drain its 100-fighter reserve so their pilots must warp in from their base Xenon Rift with double the respawn wait. Destroy every enemy capital ship to win the rift. No match clock.

↗

Move the front line

Choose your side once per Rift Cycle—your faction holds until a capital falls and the galaxy re-forms. Pick an open front on the galactic map and drop into its battle. Victories capture and lock rifts, opening adjacent fronts. Bots fill empty fighter slots and keep battles moving when pilots are away.

↗

Learn offline

Practice a 12v12 Fleet Battle, Team Deathmatch, or a full Offline Rift War against bots without an account or server connection. Track both fleets on the visual fleet-strip HUD and ship rings, while the ship-computer announcer calls out carrier attacks, reactor blasts, and forming gravity wells.

THREE SHIPS / THREE WAYS TO FIGHT

Find your fighter.

Interceptor

Fast and light. Pick your angle, strike, and slip away.

PRIMARY / PULSE SCATTER

Fires a three-bolt close-range spread (6° fan, 40 u range) built for fast hit-and-run passes.

SPECIAL / PHASE DASH

Dash in your movement direction to reposition while taking reduced damage. You cannot fire during the dash.

Striker

Balanced speed and firepower. A good place to start.

PRIMARY / PLASMA CANNON

Rapid, accurate single-bolt plasma fire (45 u range) for steady mid-range dogfight pressure.

SPECIAL / WEAPON OVERCHARGE

Temporarily increase your cannon’s fire rate (1.6×) to press the attack.

Dreadnought

Heavy armor and shields. Trade speed for staying power.

PRIMARY / RAILGUN

High-velocity, long-range heavy rounds (110 u/s, 55 u range) that hit hard from outside lighter fighters’ reach.

SPECIAL / PHOTON TORPEDOES

Launch a three-torpedo salvo that deals 5× damage to capital ships, turrets, and launch bays.

All three ships are available in practice and online. Balance and visuals are still evolving.

Can I play without other players?

Yes. Practice runs offline against bots without signing in—either a single 12v12 battle or a full Offline Rift War across the galaxy. Online, bots fill open fighter slots so you can join even when few people are playing.

Does the online galaxy keep going when I leave?

Yes. The dedicated galaxy saves its war state in the database and runs battles with bots while pilots are away. Your game does not need to stay open. Offline practice is separate from the shared online war.

What if I cannot join?

Use the current playtest build and check the galaxy status shown on the Rift War button in the main menu. If a front closes after its battle or a connection drops, return to the map or menu and join an open front. Follow the first-launch instructions supplied with your build.
