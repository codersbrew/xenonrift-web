BEFORE YOU DROP IN

Good questions.

Still stuck? Ask for help ↗

Can I play without other players?

Yes. Practice runs offline against bots, and online arenas use bots to fill open team slots. The experimental offline Rift War also runs without a server.

Is the galaxy a persistent online world?

Not yet. Online servers currently host standalone arenas; their rift state resets on server restart. The 15-rift galactic map is an offline prototype that resets when you quit. Shared online galaxy progression is future work.

Can I play on iPhone without a controller?

Not yet. Gameplay requires a paired Bluetooth controller, such as Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, or MFi. Menu taps work, but touch flight controls are not implemented.

Why does my Mac block the app?

Development builds are not signed or notarized. Only open a build you trust. Follow the instructions supplied with your playtest build; macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security after you try to open it. Don’t disable Gatekeeper globally.

Why can’t I join the server?

Check the address and confirm the playtest server is running. Your client must match the server’s build. If you lose connection, return to the menu and rejoin; automatic reconnect is not available yet. Offline Practice still works.

How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Email playtest feedback with your device, build version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots help. For website issues, use the public website issue tracker ↗.
